How to install CoinBazaar

Android requires a few quick confirmations when installing an app outside the Play Store.

Download

Tap "Download CoinBazaar.apk" and wait for the download to finish.

Open the file

Open it from the download notification or your Downloads folder.

Allow installation

If prompted, enable "Install unknown apps" for your browser, then go back.

Tap Install

Confirm the installation, then open CoinBazaar and sign up.

Note: Android may show a security notice because the app is installed outside the Play Store. This is standard for APK installs. Download only from this official page.
